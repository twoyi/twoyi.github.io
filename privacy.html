Privacy policy

For the Twoyi app (iOS and Android) and the twoyi.app website · Updated 18 November 2026

In one sentence: Twoyi has no accounts, contains no ad or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not upload the apps and data inside Twoyi to us. Apps inside Twoyi go online on their own, as on any phone.

1. Data that stays on your device

The Android system inside Twoyi, and the apps you install there, the accounts you sign in to and the data they create, are stored in Twoyi's app directory. Deleting or uninstalling Twoyi deletes them too.

2. iOS version

  • The app itself sends no requests to our servers, does not check for updates, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • Twoyi asks iOS for camera, microphone, location or motion access only when an Android app inside Twoyi uses it, and passes what iOS provides to that Android app. You can turn this off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Logs are packed only when you tap “Share Logs”, and go to the app you choose in the share sheet.

3. Android version

The Android version and Twoyi Twin are the same app, so the Twoyi Twin privacy policy applies. The points about Twoyi itself:

  • When you open the app and when you tap “Check for updates”, it reads version information from twoyi.ai; when you install Google services in settings, it reads a download configuration from twoyi.app and downloads the package from GitHub. These requests carry no account or device identifier.
  • Camera, microphone, location, Bluetooth and notification permissions are requested when needed, to provide them to the apps inside Twoyi.
  • Only when you use Twoyi Twin (AI) is task-related screen content sent to the model provider you configured; see the policy above.

4. Apps inside Twoyi

Apps you install inside Twoyi go online directly; their own privacy policies govern how they handle data.

5. This website

twoyi.app is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, analytics or ads. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operations under the GitHub privacy statement. Download links point to dl.twoyi.ai.

6. Children

Twoyi is not directed at children under 14.

7. Changes and contact

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page and post a note in the Telegram channel. For any question, email twsxtd@gmail.com.
